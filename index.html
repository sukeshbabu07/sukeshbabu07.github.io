Research Focus

Drug Discovery

Leveraging computational approaches to identify and optimize novel therapeutic compounds, focusing on rational drug design and structure-activity relationships.

Bioinformatics

Utilizing computational tools to model molecular interactions between photosensitizers and biological targets, predicting binding affinities and cellular mechanisms for effective PDT.

Photodynamic Therapy (PDT)

Investigating molecules and materials acting as photosensitizers, understanding their photophysical properties, and optimizing them for targeted cancer therapy.

Molecular Modelling & Docking

Applying computational techniques to simulate 3D molecular structures and perform molecular docking studies to evaluate drug-receptor interactions and binding affinities.
