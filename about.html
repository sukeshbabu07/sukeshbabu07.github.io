About

A passionate and driven researcher in the field of chemistry with a strong focus on Computational Chemistry, Molecular Modelling, Molecular Docking, drug discovery, drug formulation, and Molecular Dynamics. Research involves metal-based ligand docking, adding protein residues, and utilizing advanced computational tools for chemical analysis and drug discovery for cancer. This reflects a deep curiosity and commitment to solving real-world challenges through science and chemistry.

Coming from humble beginnings, a strong belief is held in continuous learning, self-improvement, and resilience. Over time, robust skills have been cultivated in AutoDock Tools, Discovery Studio, and molecular modeling, coupled with a deep understanding of chemical principles.

Beyond academics, there is a dedication to guiding students and youngsters by providing insights and education on the fundamentals and advancements in chemistry. Valuing creativity and balance, interests include music, reading, and exploring new knowledge. The aspiration is to contribute meaningfully to projects that merge computational insights with experimental innovation, aiming to develop sustainable solutions for society in energy, materials, and pharmaceuticals.

Let’s Connect: If there is a shared passion for chemical research, computational modeling, or innovative applications in science using computational methods, feel free to reach out. There is always an eagerness to learn and collaborate.
